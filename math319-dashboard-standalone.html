Stage 3: Integer Linear Programming (ILP) Formulation

Complete mathematical specification connecting cost minimization & engagement maximization

Operations Research Model

1. Binary Decision Variables:

yi,d,t ∈ {0, 1}, xd,t = Σi yi,d,t ∈ ℤ+

where yi,d,t = 1 if tutor i is assigned on day d during hour block t (enforcing the rule that one tutor cannot cover more than 1 man-hour per shift).

2. Dual Objective Functions:

(A) Maximize Engagement / Minimize Queue: Min Σd,t [ α·Wd,t+1 + β·Overlookedd,t ]
(B) Cost Efficiency: Min Σi,d,t (Wagei · yi,d,t)

3. System Constraints:

  • Service & Queue Stability: μ · xd,t ≥ λd,t during peak blocks so wait time stays below 8–10 minutes.
  • Staffing Tier Contracts: Σd,t yi,d,t = Hi (20 hrs/wk for the 6 GAs; 10 hrs/wk for Donnie, Mariah, Taylor; 8 hrs/wk for Joe & student workers).
  • Subject Competency Coverage: Σi ∈ Cs μ · yi,d,t ≥ λd,t,s for each subject s ∈ {Alg, Trig, Precalc, Calc, Stats}.
  • Room & Coverage Bounds: 1 ≤ xd,t ≤ Cmax for all operating hours (Mon–Thu, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
