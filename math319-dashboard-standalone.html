Project 1 Formulation

1. M/M/c Erlang-C Queueing Formulation:

λt = λstudent × Nt = 4 × Nt,    at = &frac{λt}{μ} = &frac{λt}{20},    ρt = &frac{at}{ct} = &frac{λt}{ct · 20}
P(Wait) = C(c, a) = &frac{ rac{a^c}{c!(1 - ρ)} }{ \sum_{k=0}^{c-1} rac{a^k}{k!} + rac{a^c}{c!(1 - ρ)} }   ( ho < 1),    Wq = &frac{P(Wait)}{cμ - λ} × 60 ext{ min}

When ρt ≥ 1.0, arrivals exceed service capacity and the queue is unstable / overflowing (Wq = 999 min). Minimum tutors for target wait is the smallest integer c ≥ 1 such that ρ < 1 and Wq(c) < 3.0 minutes.

2. Square-Root Staffing Law:

crec = ⌈ a + β √a ⌉    where β = 1.0 (Good Service) or 2.0 (Very Good Service)

Demonstrates economies of scale in queueing systems: as student traffic grows by a factor of 4, the safety buffer only needs to grow by a factor of 2 (√4 = 2).

3. Shift Template Integer Programming Model:

ct = Σp xp · covers(p, t),    Σp ∈ GA xp = 9,    Σp ∈ SI xp ≤ 4,    ct ≥ 1
ext{Minimize Weighted Avg Wait} = &frac{ \sum_{t=1}^{36} λt · Wq,t }{ \sum_{t=1}^{36} λt }

Why weight by λt? Weighting by requests-for-help (λt) ensures that the average wait time reported reflects the actual experience of students seeking assistance, rather than weighting quiet morning hours equally with high-demand midday rushes.
